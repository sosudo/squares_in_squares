## Coverage and the actual novelty

The supported first experiment is persistent optimization of complete
rectangular gadgets, followed by exact recomposition. Start with the four
explicit types 5×17+7 (92 units), 7×19+13 (146), 7×20+14 (154), and 8×21+13
(181). The next two types, if the first experiment transfers to whole cases,
are 8×22+14 and 9×21+8. Their actual prior chain uses, source banks and hashes
are in `artifacts/strategy-2224-coverage-v1/chain-inventory.json`.

This is a new combination of search state and domain, not a claim that these
rectangles or their mutations were never optimized. The earlier coherent CPU
pilot ran 36 independent trials in 39.30 seconds on the first three types,
including 12/24-unit rotations, translations, reflections, band slides and
shears. It found only a 4.4e-7 fractional-side improvement and preserved a
0.00915-wider alternative. Native soft/aspect and contact campaigns also
searched these types. FTMC repeatedly stripped/refilled upright units and
admitted certified endpoint variants; it did not keep an imperfect complete
rectangle as its persistent Metropolis state. The corrected persistent GPU
programs so far used complete square containers (including the closed n65
pilot), while the rectangular-wall GPU program was a short whole-n335
wall-path experiment. Repeating n65, n335, or the unchanged 7033-pose
composition union is excluded here.

The conditional coverage is substantial but not a forecast. In the saved
35-chain library, lowering all three leading OLD recipe fractional sides by
0.05 while preserving every required interface would cover only 0.01322;
the leading six would cover 0.02008. Lowering all 16 old families by 0.10
would cover 0.06705. These decrements are from old recipe values, not current
minima, and every tied partner/profile must remain compatible. They cannot
be added to public-table gaps. The dated scalar inventory needs roughly 82
of its largest individual reference differences to approach the new target.
Three routine scalar improvements cannot credibly promise the required gain.

## Experiment 1: complete-rectangle persistent discovery

**Inputs.** Freeze the newest orientation-fair minimum and up to eight
geometrically different, exactly typed representatives per family, combining
the original winning interfaces, combined FTMC archive, and strict native
archives. Preserve the source wall phases and wider variants up to f+0.02
when they carry a different contact/profile pattern. Do not mistake thousands
of float-byte variants for independent basins. A bounded D4/unit-assignment
and contact/profile audit must report the actual number of representatives;
if only one group exists, label that limitation. No old archive is resumed
as an overwrite of the current best.

**State and objective.** Every square, upright or tilted, remains an
independent unit-square variable. A family has explicit fixed physical box
W=w+f_target, H=h+f_target, exact count N=w*h+d, and a fresh all-pair/four-wall
physical energy for acceptance. The existing symmetric-tie FP64 rectangular
quench already accepts two-dimensional boxes and remains at 2000 steps.
There is no core-count gate, row-capacity screen, detached refill or recentering
of an extracted tilted core. Fixed-box chains use f_target=f_current minus
0.005/0.02/0.05, with half the chains at 0.005 and one quarter at each deeper
cut. Energies are never compared across different boxes.

**Population and moves.** Use 64 independent chains initially, balanced over
the exact input groups and preserving at least 25% current-reference starts.
The four existing whole-pose moves remain an auditable starting policy;
within a narrow rectangle their radius can cover the entire short dimension.
Their prior 36 native endpoint trials are not a positive result for the
persistent process. A reviewed public seam operator can be added as a
separate declared treatment after root's released-source assessment; it must
not silently enter the baseline. Both current and best states survive, and
native finishing takes both minimum-energy and contact/profile-diverse BEST
states. The sustained run's three diverse-slot winners and one weaker-cut
winner justify preserving these channels without proving a causal ablation.

**Finite resource plan.** One dual-T4 job has two queues of two families,
45 minutes search per family, and a whole bound of 120 minutes with an
explicit 20-minute startup, controls, native-finishing and finalization
reserve. Each family has its own initialized absolute epoch, cooling during
the final 10 minutes, a quench-admission tail guard and actual-size controls.
Before freezing the batch choice, one bounded actual-N181 B64/B128 comparison
must check energy/gradient/last-row identity, memory and paired endpoint
variation. Use B64 unless B128 provides a material measured benefit without
consuming the reserve; there is no production FP32 path. A later larger
allocation is conditional on real propagation, not on a lower raw energy.

**Finishing and propagation.** At fixed 10-minute checkpoints and termination,
select a bounded union of lowest energy, coarse-distinct geometry and useful
incoming/outgoing profile states; preserve every exact selected initializer.
Use the existing rectangular native encoding and original ALM45/pit100/flip16
finish, with explicit per-trial bounds. Retain all fully certified outputs,
including worse scalar variants. Calculate conservative bounds for both
rectangle spans, not merely the enclosing square side. Recompute actual
profiles and run the existing exact-chain replay and finite partner screen
immediately. Whole-case official raw, ordinary-pandas and independent gates
remain the authority. A small component loss alone is not a score gain.

**Scale decision.** The positive criterion is a material certified component
reduction, preferably at least 0.01 in f, plus at least two complete-case
improvements totaling 0.001 or more. This is a predeclared engineering bar,
not a prediction. If the first four types only recreate old minima or their
new profiles are incompatible, do not spend the remaining quota extending
them unchanged. A positive result expands the measured compatible family
portfolio toward the 16-type map and then newly generated types; it does not
justify extrapolating the entire 0.067 counterfactual.

## Experiment 2: vacancy relocation from a feasible rectangle

This is a lower-confidence, materially different proposal, and requires a
small actual control before any long allocation. It is an alternative to
another run on the failed n498/n587 composition scaffolds. Those scaffolds
were already missing one feasible full-size placement; 1056 returned size-1
birth proposals and eight native finishes failed. Here the starting object
is a fully certified current N-unit rectangle, with a known feasible unit
endpoint before contraction.

Choose a unit at a tilted/upright seam, permute that exact row to the last
index, and use the already reviewed single-variable-size physical kernel to
shrink it from 1 to 0.05 while all other units can move. At small size, relocate
it to a distinct low-overlap site selected from wall/contact candidates,
then regrow it through a fixed schedule to exactly 1. Only the last unit has
variable size, so the existing unequal-size tie rule remains applicable;
rectangular walls still need an exact two-coordinate adapter and controls.
All squares outside the seam may move, so this does not freeze a radius-2.25
or radius-3.25 cavity. Start from ordinary current rectangles, not the old
497/586 one-deficit scaffolds.

Each size stage resets its optimizer history and computes a new size-specific
energy. Never compare energies across sizes. Only the final all-unit pose is
eligible for persistent fixed-box acceptance or native finishing. Retain exact
row permutation, selected unit, old/new sites, size schedule and unit-endpoint
checks. An actual unit-source shrink/return control must recover valid N at
the source box, and a relocated control must show unlabeled geometric/contact
change rather than a pure permutation. All eventual admissions use the same
full gates as experiment 1.

If those controls are positive, a finite dual-T4 one-hour paired experiment
uses one proven reusable rectangle on both devices: ordinary full-unit
persistence versus the same treatment with a declared fraction of relocation
cycles. Match the input groups, fixed boxes, seeds and wall-clock allocation;
report fewer completed proposals in the more expensive continuation arm.
Its success criterion is an actually recomposed gain beyond the unit-only
arm or a new compatible count/profile construction, not a softer intermediate
energy. Failure to recover useful all-unit endpoints closes this finite scope.
This single-vacancy mechanism still may be insufficient for a wall-spanning
multi-unit rearrangement; it is not evidence that the broader reconstruction
problem is solved. Root/anchor's released seam and joint-incidence reviews
may supply a better second treatment, and take precedence over inventing a
large new integer/continuous framework without a working control.

raw proposal was our earlier implementation choice. For these new jobs,
predeclare full current/best/proposal packets every 32 rounds, at phase
boundaries, on a new zero-energy endpoint and at termination; save every

acceptance, operator and RNG identity records for all rounds. The audit must
state that full transition arrays are sampled, not claim a complete replay
of every unretained state. Test interrupted publication and last-checkpoint
recovery before launch. The measured self-contained exact block codec can
reduce sampled packet sizes further, but is not necessary to avoid deliberate
storage pauses. Old published raw archives remain untouched.

with actual memory/storage/clock reports. Both experiments are offline,

actual-device/numerical/retention review. Neither mutates the canonical
portfolio or submits. Root owns local whole-case merges and the below-22.24
